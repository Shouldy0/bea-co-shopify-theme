In this video, I'm going to show you 10 websites that you may not know of that you can get free traffic from. Okay, and these are all powerful. These are all kind of up and coming, too. So, like if you catch them now, you'll be able to ride the trend and basically get a lot of free traffic. Below this video, I'll have this little document that'll show you the 10 hidden traffic sources that you can get.

Before you really start sending tons of traffic, you have to optimize your website and you have to optimize your offer. Like just telling people, "Hey, go here." isn't going to turn them into a customer. You need to basically come up with your irresistible offer. The easiest way to do this is basically think about what your website offers and what you sell, and then you come up with your call to action, which would be like, "Get A, B, and C without X, Y, and Z."

So, like for mine, basically cuz we sell traffic, "Get a ton of traffic and customers fast without dancing on TikTok, making content, doing complicated SEO, or ever having to become an expert marketer." So, come up with your call to action, and then make sure your website is optimized so that it will convert people.

All right, next step is now we're going to start getting into traffic.

1. **band.us** — community platform where people create groups around shared interests. How to get traffic: join groups in your related niche, share helpful tips and resources, create your own band community, include your website in your profile, share useful content instead of constantly promoting. It's not as strict as Reddit, so lots of opportunity. Real goal: build a community and become a trusted resource. Build lots of communities on lots of platforms, see which one grows, double down on that one.

2. **We Are Eight** — kind of like TikTok without all the blockages. A social platform with much less competition than mainstream social media. How: post short-form videos, share tips and tutorials, tell stories about your business, add your website and LinkedIn profile, post consistently. If you're already posting shorts/TikToks/Reels, post on this newer platform and capture attention while competition is low.

3. **Lemon8** — built by the same people who made TikTok. Kind of like Pinterest meets TikTok. Works really good for makeup, health and fitness, and lifestyle angles. Post on Lemon8 if those are in your niche.

4. **Medium** — powerful because you can rank on Google for articles with Medium, and you get link juice/backlinks from Medium. One of the easiest places to publish articles. Publish helpful articles, solve problems in your niche, include relevant links back to your website, republish content you've already created. Backlinks from Medium are really good.

5. **Hashnode** — blogging platform popular among developers and technology creators. Publish tutorials, share public breakdowns, document your journey, link back to your website, build authority. For AI/tech niches.

6. **dev.to** — social media community for developers, 3+ million developers. Join their challenges, post articles, add videos, get backlinks, become known as someone who provides value. Focused on development, AI startups, technology.

7. **Vocal Media** — allows users to publish articles across multiple categories. Find top stories, latest stories, communities. Great way to find communities of people interested in what you do. Build relationships, post a lot of value. Become a big fish in a small pond.

8. **Peerlist** — like Product Hunt meets LinkedIn. Launch your business, get ranked, look for jobs, post articles. Up-and-coming professional place to post your content, build a community, get in before everybody else jumps on the bandwagon.

9. **Udemy** — gets tons and tons of traffic every single day. The hack: create a FREE course that gives a lot of value, then plug your product inside the course, driving people to your sales page. You can get a huge email list because everybody who signs up for your course, you get their email. Great for free traffic + free emails + sending to your offer page.

(The video also mentions StoryFire as an article/story platform in the repurposing system.)

**The system:** create really good articles, then publish them everywhere. Don't use AI to write your articles, use it to edit and make the process faster. One piece of content → post on Medium, Hashnode, dev.to, Vocal Media, Lemon8 (as a carousel), We Are Eight (as a post), StoryFire (article), Peerlist, Band (discussion), then maybe a Udemy lesson.

**Final tip:** don't focus on going viral, focus on publishing consistently. A small amount of traffic from 10 different sources often outperforms relying on a single platform. Goal: create valuable content, distribute it everywhere, capture visitors, and turn traffic into customers.
