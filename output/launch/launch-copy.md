## Presentazione del prodotto
Some days, it helps to have somewhere to put the questions. Bea's Calm-Alone Kit brings together a practical guide, a fillable workbook and a quick reference. Keep track of the setting, the small observations and what you'd like to discuss with your professional. Three English PDFs, made to read, save and use at your own pace. Explore the kit and preview the pages before you choose.

## Post dimostrativo del workbook
“What happened?” is a more useful note than “good” or “bad”. Our session page has space for the planned action, what you actually saw, uncertainty and your next question. You can type into it or print a blank copy. The workbook is an organisational tool, not a clinical assessment.

## Post trasparenza
A real dog. A practical workbook. Honest limits. Bea's Calm-Alone Kit is owner-created educational support. It does not diagnose your dog or promise a cure. If separation is difficult, professional advice can help shape an individual plan. Our aim is to help you bring clearer notes to that conversation.
